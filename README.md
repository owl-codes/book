# The Wildtongue Chronicles

**Book One: The Choir of Erythis-3**

A lone agent roams the furthest reaches of the galaxy — specialist in futuristic weaponry, fluent in the languages of every living creature, from the wolves of Old Earth to the luminous hive-minds of the Crellan Nebula. When a distress call pulls him to an ocean world the Union classified as empty, he finds something the galaxy was never supposed to know about: an ancient species, caged and starving, holding a seal against something worse.

When diplomacy fails, the weapons speak. When weapons fail, the animals listen.
